Morph support

Let’s get your file moving.

For the fastest help, include your iPhone model, iOS version, source format, target format, and what happened. Please do not attach private files.

Email support

Before contacting support

  • Confirm the source opens normally in Photos or Files.
  • Check Morph’s access under Settings → Apps → Morph.
  • Make sure the iPhone has enough free storage for the result.
  • Try a smaller file and note both the source and target formats.
  • Try again after closing and reopening Morph.

Files and privacy

Morph version 1.0 performs conversions and edits on your device. It does not upload your files or collect app usage data. Read the complete Privacy Policy.

Deleting Morph data

Remove individual results from the Morph library, or delete Morph to remove its local app sandbox. Copies already exported to Photos, Files, or another app must be deleted from that destination separately.
