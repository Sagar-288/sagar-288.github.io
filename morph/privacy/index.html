Privacy without the fine print.

Morph version 1.0 works on your device, requires no account, and does not send your files or app usage data to us.

No file uploadsConversions and edits happen on your iPhone.
No accountNo name, email, password, or profile is required.
No trackingNo advertising SDKs or cross-app identifiers.

1. Scope

This policy applies to Morph for iOS version 1.0. Morph is designed to convert and edit files on your device without requiring an account.

2. Data collection

Morph version 1.0 does not collect data from the app. It does not send your files, file names, file contents, conversion history, device identifiers, location, diagnostics, or usage information to Morph or to third parties.

Morph contains no advertising SDK, cross-app tracking, or third-party analytics. It does not use Apple’s advertising identifier and does not create user profiles.

3. Files and edits

File conversion and editing happen on your device. Source files are read only after you choose them. Results are saved in Morph’s private app storage or to a destination you explicitly select, such as Photos or the Files app.

Morph does not upload or retain a server copy of your source files or results.

4. Information stored on your device

Morph may store the following information locally in its app sandbox:

  • converted files you keep in the Morph library;
  • app preferences and home-screen layout; and
  • technical conversion outcomes used only by the app itself.

This local information is not transmitted off the device. You can remove saved files from within Morph.

5. Permissions

Morph may request access to Photos, Camera, or Microphone only when you use a feature that needs it. The permission is used to read or create the item you requested. You can change these permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

6. Retention and deletion

Morph version 1.0 has no server-side user data to retain or delete. Locally stored data remains until you remove it in Morph or delete the app. Deleting Morph removes its app sandbox, except for copies you previously exported to Photos, Files, or another app.

7. Third parties

Morph uses Apple system frameworks and bundled software components to provide app functionality. Version 1.0 does not use those components to send app data to analytics providers, advertising networks, or other third parties.

8. Children

Morph does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

9. Changes to this policy

If a future version introduces an optional online service or data collection, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before that version is released. Where required, Morph will ask for consent before sending data or files.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to skalsaria288@gmail.com. You can also visit Morph Support.
