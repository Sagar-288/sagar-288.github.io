Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play?

Tap the screen to reverse your orbit direction. Dodge obstacles, collect power-ups, and survive as long as you can. The longer you last, the faster it gets!

How do I unlock spaceships and ring themes?

Collect star coins during gameplay or purchase coin packs from the in-game shop. Use coins to unlock new spaceships and ring themes from the customization menus.

How do I restore my purchases?

Go to the in-game Shop and tap "Restore Purchases" to recover any previously purchased coin packs.

How do I save my progress across devices?

Sign in with your account from the main menu. Your progress, unlocks, and high scores will sync across all your devices.

The game is running slowly. What can I do?

Close other apps running in the background and restart Orbit Dash. The game is optimized for smooth 60 FPS performance on most devices.
